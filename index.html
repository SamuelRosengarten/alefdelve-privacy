Alef Delve: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 (for version 1.2)

Alef Delve collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (words learned, streaks, game saves, settings) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it from your phone. A backup file you saved yourself, or a backup of your phone, may still hold a copy.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to read a line aloud, the app asks permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Recognition of your Hebrew is done on your phone. Recordings of how you say a word stay on your phone and are not uploaded.

Home Screen widget

The "This Shabbat" widget shows this week's Torah portion and your streak. It reads that information from the app on your own phone.

Optional answers in the guide

When you first open the app, an optional guide asks a few questions: whether you are Jewish or not, your community and prayer book, your goals, and your city. All of them can be skipped. Your answers are kept only on your phone, are never sent anywhere, and are used only to suggest where to begin, how to explain things and, from your goals, which new words come first. The one exception: if you send a report of a mistake (below), you can choose to include your community and prayer book. You can change or erase them at any time (Me, then Settings, then the guide), and deleting the app removes them.

Your time zone and city

The app reads your phone's time zone to pick a nearby city, which sets Shabbat and holiday times and which calendar (Israel or the diaspora) is used. It does not use your location, does not ask for location permission, and sends nothing anywhere.

Reports from rabbis and advanced learners

A section for rabbis, teachers and advanced learners lets them report a mistake. A report is an e-mail you write and send yourself from your own mail app. The app adds what you type in the form, the role you pick and where in the app you opened it, plus the app version and the iOS version. Your community and prayer book from the guide are added only if you tick that box. It adds no device identifier, location, city, name or progress, and it sends nothing by itself.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new policy will be posted here and the app's store page will be updated before the change ships.
